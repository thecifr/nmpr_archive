Preserving what would otherwise be lost.

ASTRA is a volunteer-run archive. We collect, catalogue, and preserve unresolved records — testimony, documentation, and accounts that have nowhere else to live. We are not an investigative body. We do not solve cases. We keep them.

The archive is maintained by a small group of anonymous volunteers. All records are submitted by the public and presented as-is.
